Invoice Logo

Paste your logo's direct link (PNG/JPG) — it appears at the top of the invoice. Leave empty to show only the ADATA CLOUD text.

Slide to make the logo bigger or smaller on printed invoices, statements and cash book.

Move the logo to the left side, middle, or right side of the invoice top.

Invoice Layout

Show Qty column
You sell software/services, not physical products — keep Qty OFF (default). Turn it ON later if you start selling products; the Qty column will return on invoices.

Print Style — Invoices & Reports

Controls EVERYTHING that prints — invoices, client statements and cash book. Printed text looks thin or unreadable? Increase Text Darkness, thicken the writing, or switch to pure Black & White.

Print looks too thin? Slide up to darken every word & number (100% = pure black). Keep at 0% for the original light look.

Black & White Mode
All colored accents become neutral dark gray — perfect for black & white laser printers. Status marks (PAID/DUE) stay readable.
